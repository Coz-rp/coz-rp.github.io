One preset for Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and GLM: a block meant for some models starts with a coloured dot for each of them (🟠 Claude, 🔵 Gemini, 🟢 DeepSeek, 🟣 GLM), so you switch on the ones with your colour and off the rest.

Extension

Worldhopper Engine

The part a preset can't do. Tell it what kind of metaphysics a character uses, and it keeps the rules, the bodies and the prose straight in that character's chats. It pairs with the presets above and works with any other.

Only what you're into. Every mode is opt-in, picked per character, and nothing is on until you pick it. If mind control is your thing, pick Mind Control and that's all you'll ever get: no possession rules, no body swaps, no boxes or cards for modes you didn't choose. Mix as many or as few as you like, and change them any time.
In SillyTavern, open Extensions → Install extension, and paste:
https://github.com/Coz-rp/SillyTavern-Worldhopper
Then open Extensions → Worldhopper Engine → Settings → Models and pick a connection profile for each job: a local or cheap, fast model, not your roleplay model. Tested on SillyTavern 1.18. Source and README on GitHub ↗
Keep it fast and cheap. Give the Engine its own model, separate from the one you roleplay with. Its jobs are small background ones, and the Ledger runs after every single reply, so a local model (KoboldCpp, Ollama and the like) or a cheap, fast API model from the Flash, Haiku or mini tier does them well and costs next to nothing. On your big roleplay model they'd work too, but every background call would cost big-model prices, and your next reply waits for the Ledger to finish.

Codex modes

19 modes, from possession and skinsuits to hypnosis, mind control, dolls and time stop. Their rules switch on only in the chats that use them, from a lorebook the Engine installs and keeps updated.

Body Ledger

In possession-type chats, a model you choose records after each reply who is in which body and whether you know, then feeds it back so nothing slips. A body stays on it until the story lets it go.

Mind Book

Hypnosis, mind control and altered perception each keep their own record: trance stages, suggestions and triggers; what a controller set in someone's will; who can't perceive whom. A trigger word you say fires on cue.

Strip and body roster

Every borrowed body at a glance above the chat: teal when you're the one inside, violet when someone else is. Run several at once and each gets a colour-coded card that shows what it's doing and starts a line for it.

Moment boxes

A takeover, an absorption, someone going under, a will taking hold, time stopping: each mode's big moment gets its own styled box in the chat.

Card tools

Make card turns a Card Builder chat into a character, modes and all. Check card reviews any card for the things that trip models up.

WH Editor. Its own extension now, and it works with any setup: a rule pass flags stock phrases, "not X, but Y" framing and chopped dialogue, and a cheap model rewrites only those lines. One tap brings the original back. Get it on GitHub ↗

Setup

Getting set up

Five minutes, start to finish. You'll need SillyTavern connected to a Chat Completion source (Anthropic, OpenRouter, NanoGPT, Google AI Studio and the like).

  1. Import a preset. Open AI Response Configuration (the sliders icon), choose Import, and pick the file. When SillyTavern asks whether to allow the preset's regex, allow it. Pick your model as usual; the preset won't change it.
  2. Install the Engine. Extensions → Install extension, paste https://github.com/Coz-rp/SillyTavern-Worldhopper. Then, under its Settings → Models, give it its own model, separate from your roleplay one: a local model or a cheap, fast API model keeps everything quick and costs next to nothing.
  3. Pick a character's modes. Open a character and tap the pen under Modes, or let the wand suggest some. Every section of the Engine's panel has a (?) that explains it in a few lines.
  4. Optional: the Card Builder. Import it, build a card with it, then use Make card from the wand menu.
